# Precision Neuroscience

> Precision Neuroscience is a New York City brain-computer interface company founded in 2021 by
> Benjamin Rapoport, Michael Mager, Demetrios Papageorgiou and Mark Hettick, with additional sites
> in Santa Clara, California and Addison, Texas. It builds the Layer 7 Cortical Interface — a
> thin-film microelectrode array that rests on the surface of the cortex without penetrating brain
> tissue, and that is designed to be removable and upgradable. Precision sells a regulated medical
> device, not software services, and does not run a developer program or publish an API.

## About

The Layer 7 Cortical Interface received FDA 510(k) clearance authorizing commercial use for
recording, monitoring and stimulating on the surface of the brain for implantation durations of up
to 30 days. The company reports 95+ clinical study participants and 15+ active hospital
partnerships, including Mount Sinai, WVU Medicine, Penn Medicine, Johns Hopkins Medicine, Mass
General Brigham, Beth Israel Deaconess, Northwestern Medicine, Emory Healthcare, Barrow Neurological
Institute, NYU, UChicago Medicine, Michigan Medicine and Thomas Jefferson University. In January
2026 Precision Neuroscience and Medtronic announced a strategic partnership.
